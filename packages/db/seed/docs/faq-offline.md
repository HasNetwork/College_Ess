---
title: Does College Ess work offline?
kind: faq
audience: user
order: 0
minRole: student
published: true
---
Partly. What's already on your device stays readable: this guide, and whatever College Ess last synced for you. Anything that needs College Ess itself waits for a connection, such as signing in, saving a setting or uploading a photo.

On campus Wi-Fi, "offline" often means the network's sign-in page is waiting. Open any website, sign in to the Wi-Fi, and College Ess carries on. More in [Offline Support & Synchronization](/guide/offline).
