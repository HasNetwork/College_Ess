# Permissions & Roles

Almost everyone in College Ess is a student. A few people also help run it, and what each of them can do is always tied to one place: a club, a college, or College Ess as a whole.

## Roles

- **Student**: everyone. Your own things, such as your reminders, settings and photo, are yours alone.
- **College admin**: keeps one college's shared information right and reviews the changes students suggest for it.
- **Platform admin**: looks after College Ess itself.
- **Club coordinator**: a student who runs one club's page and events. This role arrives with clubs.

## One example

An admin of one college can't see or change anything that belongs to another college. A club coordinator can manage their own club, and no other.
