---
title: Why did a reminder arrive at 7 AM instead of at night?
kind: faq
audience: user
order: 3
minRole: student
published: true
---
Quiet hours hold reminders between 10 PM and 7 AM, on your own clock, and deliver them at 7 AM. Nothing is dropped. To get them at night, open **Settings** and switch off **Quiet hours**.
