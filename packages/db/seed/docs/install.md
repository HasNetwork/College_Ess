# Installing College Ess (iOS & Android)

College Ess runs in your browser, and you can add it to your Home Screen so it opens like an app.

## Why Install?

- **Reminders on an iPhone or iPad.** Apple delivers them only to apps on your Home Screen, not to Safari tabs.
- **Quicker to reach.** It opens full-screen, without the browser's address bar.
- **Works on bad Wi-Fi.** What's already on your device stays readable.

## How to Install on iOS (iPhone / iPad)

1. Open College Ess in **Safari**.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing upward at the bottom of the screen).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Confirm by tapping **Add** in the top-right corner.
5. Open College Ess from your Home Screen, then turn on **Reminders on this device** in **Settings**.

## How to Install on Android

1. Open College Ess in **Chrome** (or your preferred browser).
2. Look for the **Install** prompt banner at the bottom or open the browser menu (three dots in top-right).
3. Tap **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
4. Confirm installation. The app icon will appear on your home screen and app drawer.
