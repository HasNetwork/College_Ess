On an iPhone or iPad, reminders arrive only when College Ess is on your Home Screen. Open it in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, and open College Ess from there. Then turn on **Reminders on this device** in **Settings**. Step by step: [Installing College Ess](/guide/install).
