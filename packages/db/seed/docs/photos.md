# Photos & Media Storage

To add a profile photo, open **Dashboard** and tap **Change photo**. Wardrobe photos arrive with Drip.

## If an Upload Fails
Your photo stays on screen with the reason, for example that you're offline or that the app's photo storage isn't taking uploads right now. Tap **Try again** later; the photo you picked isn't lost.

## Privacy
- **Private to you.** Nobody else can see the photos in your account.
- **Straight to storage.** Your photo goes from your device directly to College Ess's photo storage, through a private link that expires shortly after.

## Smaller Before It Leaves Your Phone
A phone camera photo is often 5–12 MB. College Ess resizes your profile photo to 512 pixels and compresses it on your device before uploading, so it takes a fraction of that.

## Storage Limits
- **Your space:** up to 50 MB of photos and files.
- **Per file:** profile photos up to 1 MB, wardrobe photos up to 2 MB, other attachments up to 5 MB.
- **Deleting:** a deleted photo is removed for good after a 7-day safety period. Changing your profile photo deletes the old one, so it stops counting toward your 50 MB.
