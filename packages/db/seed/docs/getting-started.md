# Getting Started

College Ess brings your college life into one app, and keeps working when campus Wi-Fi doesn't.

## What you can do today

- **Get reminders.** Open **Settings** and turn on **Reminders on this device**. They arrive on your own clock, and quiet hours hold them overnight.
- **Add a profile photo.** On the **Dashboard**, tap **Change photo**.
- **Protect your account.** On the **Dashboard**, turn on two-factor authentication with an authenticator app.
- **Put College Ess on your Home Screen.** It opens like an app, and on an iPhone it's the only way reminders arrive. See [Installing College Ess](/guide/install).

## What's coming

College Ess is built one module at a time, and each one brings its own page in this guide:

- **Loop**: your to-do list, with deadlines.
- **Rhythm**: your daily routine, class by class.
- **Drip**: your wardrobe, outfits and laundry.
- **Groove**: the habits you're building, with streaks.

The tab bar shows only what you can use today.
