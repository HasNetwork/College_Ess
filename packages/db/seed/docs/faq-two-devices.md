---
title: What happens if I change the same thing on two devices?
kind: faq
audience: user
order: 1
minRole: student
published: true
---
The change that reaches College Ess last wins. Say you rename a task on your phone while offline, then rename it again on your laptop: whichever device sends its change last decides the name, and the other change is replaced. College Ess doesn't merge the two.

Anything College Ess counts is different: each entry is saved on its own, so two devices never cancel each other out.
