# Reminders & Push Notifications

College Ess sends reminders to the phones and computers where you turn them on.

## Turning Reminders On

Reminders arrive on each phone or computer where you turn them on:

1. Open **Settings**.
2. Turn on **Reminders on this device**.
3. When your browser asks, tap **Allow**.

Do this once on every device you want reminders on. On a device someone else used before you, the switch shows off until you turn it on under your own account.

## The iOS Requirement: Must Be Installed as a PWA

On Apple iOS and iPadOS devices:
- Web Push notifications **only function** when College Ess is installed to your Home Screen as a standalone web app.
- If opened directly in Safari or Chrome browser tabs, notifications cannot be registered or delivered.
- See the [Installing College Ess](/guide/install) guide for step-by-step instructions.

## Your Time Zone

Reminders arrive at their time on your own clock: a 9:00 AM reminder comes at 9:00 AM where you are, and stays at 9:00 AM when the clocks change for daylight saving.

College Ess takes your time zone from your phone when you sign up. To change it, open **Settings** and pick one under **Time zone**. If you open College Ess on a device set to a different zone, after a trip for example, it asks whether to switch. Choose **Keep** and it won't ask again on that device.

## Quiet Hours

Quiet hours hold reminders between **10:00 PM and 7:00 AM** in your time zone, and deliver them at 7:00 AM. They're on unless you turn them off: open **Settings** and switch off **Quiet hours**.
- A reminder due at 11:30 PM arrives at 7:00 AM instead. Nothing is dropped.
